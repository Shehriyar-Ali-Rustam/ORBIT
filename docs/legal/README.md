# Legal Documents

Legal, compliance, and policy documents.

## Suggested contents

- Privacy policy drafts and revisions
- Terms of service
- Cookie policy
- GDPR / data protection notes
- Independent contractor agreements
- Non-disclosure agreements (NDA) templates
- Client contract templates
- Compliance checklists

**Note:** Signed contracts with actual client details should go in `../clients/[client-name]/` instead.
